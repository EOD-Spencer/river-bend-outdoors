About River Bend Outdoors

I started River Bend Outdoors because I enjoy getting outside, whether it’s fishing somewhere new or exploring a trail close to home. I also like telling people about the places I find, especially the things I wish somebody had told me before I went.

Of course, knowing what to expect and getting what you expected are two different things.

A Little About Me

I'm Cody. I'm a husband, dad of three, and a guy who'd rather be fishing. Between work and three kids with very different ideas of fun, getting everybody outside can take some coordination. Some days I get out on my own. Other days the whole family comes along, and the plans tend to change.

I'm also a military veteran and former first responder. My background includes land navigation, leading EOD teams, and technical rescue. It's part of how I approach planning and being outdoors, but I'd rather tell you about the places I've explored than spend much time talking about my résumé.

More Than Our Family Trips

I also like digging into what's happening closer to home. There are parks and trails around southeastern Connecticut with interesting histories, proposed improvements, changing access, or questions that deserve a better answer than a quick internet search. Sometimes the research itself is the story. Other times, it's what I find when I finally get out there.

I want to share what I can actually stand behind, whether that's a place I've visited or information I've checked carefully.

Why River Bend?

The name goes back to my grandfather, Kent Spencer Sr. He hunted with hounds in North Carolina, including one named River Bend Flag. His love of the outdoors stuck with me. River Bend Outdoors is a way of carrying a little of that history forward.

I'd love to hear from you

Found a place you think I should check out? Have thoughts on something I've shared, or a story about how your family experienced a place? I love hearing what other people notice—especially those little moments with the kids that you couldn't have planned.
