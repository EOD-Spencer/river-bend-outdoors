About River Bend Outdoors

River Bend Outdoors is a personal journal about real experiences, getting outside, and learning to be more present in everyday life.

Here you'll find firsthand stories about hiking, fishing, paddling, camping, outdoor gear, and exploring places worth visiting—especially around southeastern Connecticut. I want to share useful information about what to expect, what to bring, and what an outing is actually like with kids.

You'll also find reflections on what I'm learning through cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), marriage, fatherhood, and trying to slow down and be more present. Those stories don't have to involve an outdoor adventure to belong here. The common thread is sharing honestly, learning as I go, and offering something useful to someone else.

The Person Behind RBO

I'm Cody, a military veteran with a first-responder background, a husband, a father, and someone who genuinely enjoys being outdoors.

I started River Bend Outdoors to combine my interest in exploring places, researching the details, and spending time outside with my family. The outdoors has also given me a way to slow down, reconnect, and find balance.

The name River Bend has a personal meaning. My grandfather, Kent William Spencer Sr., was a North Carolina coon hunter whose love of the outdoors and his hounds, especially River Bend Flag, had a lasting influence on me. Naming this project River Bend Outdoors is my way of carrying a piece of that legacy forward.

I don't claim to be an expert in every outdoor activity or to have everything figured out in everyday life. I believe there is value in firsthand experience, thoughtful preparation, and being willing to say what I don't know.

When I research a location or write a practical guide, I want to answer the questions that actually matter to someone planning an outing. That means paying attention to access, parking, terrain, facilities, restrictions, and whether an experience makes sense for the people going.

RBO isn't about making every outing perfect or having all the answers. It's about exploring, paying attention, learning from experience, and sharing what might help someone else along the way.

Whether you're taking the kids for their first hike, exploring unfamiliar fishing water, or working on being more present with the people you love, I hope you'll find something here worth taking with you.
