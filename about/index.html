About River Bend Outdoors

The original article collection is under editorial review. Stories will return as they are individually approved.

River Bend Outdoors is where I share outdoor adventures, family stories, and the occasional lesson learned the hard way. You'll find fishing, kayaking, trails, and practical notes about places worth exploring, especially around southeastern Connecticut. Not every story happens outdoors.

The Guy Behind RBO

I'm Cody—a husband, dad of three, military veteran, former first responder, and a guy who'd usually rather be fishing.

Most of my early adult life was spent in the military, where structure, planning, and accomplishing the mission were part of every day. When I got out, the structure disappeared. The mindset didn't.

So yes, I may have studied satellite imagery, picked three rally points, and worked out an evacuation plan for a one-mile hike with the kids. I'm working on it.

RBO is a reason to get back to what I love, on my own or with my family, and to learn how to enjoy an outing without turning it into an operation.

Stories Worth Keeping

Some stories will be practical. Others will be funny, frustrating, or surprisingly meaningful moments from raising kids. I'm writing them partly so that, years from now, my children can see life through Dad's eyes—not just what happened, but what I was thinking.

I want to be honest without making my wife or kids the punchline. Fortunately, I give myself plenty of material.

The River Bend Name

The name is a nod to my grandfather, Kent William Spencer Sr., a North Carolina coon hunter who loved the outdoors and his hounds, especially River Bend Flag. His love for being outside helped shape mine.

If you get a useful tip, a laugh, or a reason to make a few memories of your own, I'm glad you're here.
