Map sources

Mapped paths: OpenStreetMap, data checked November 2026. Historic trail: East Lyme GIS, recorded in 2016. Land outlines: approximate mapped boundaries. The Goodwin Trail was rerouted near Darrow Pond in September 2024.

East Lyme lists the Goodwin Trail approach at 16 Mostowy Road. Follow posted signs and current town guidance for access; mapped lines do not by themselves designate public routes.
