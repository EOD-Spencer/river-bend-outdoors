The Weekend I Didn’t Rush

Ordinary moments I’m trying to experience.

This weekend wasn’t anything special on the calendar.

Friday night was a last-minute baseball game behind Flanders Elementary on the Miracle League field.

  • A bunch of kids.
  • A bunch of parents.
  • A bunch of pizza.

Everybody playing

What was supposed to be a time for the kids to practice turned into several hours of baseball, laughing, and kids running around until the sun started going down.

The field is small, which made it perfect for Corbin. Throw a helmet on him and suddenly he’s part of the game too.

Saturday morning, Corbin rode on my lap while I cut the grass. Every time we passed under a tree branch, he’d reach up trying to grab the leaves.

At one point I caught myself thinking about how much I’m going to miss that.

Not just with him.

I remember Kylie and Kaysen doing the same thing.

The difference is they’ve already outgrown it.

Saturday afternoon we ended up at the arcade. While playing air hockey, I got down to Corbin’s level and couldn’t believe how big the table looked from where he was standing.

From my perspective, it was just an air hockey table. From his perspective, it looked like an NHL arena.

Then Sunday came. A little more grass cutting, this time without Corbin riding along, and most of the afternoon at the lake.

Nothing remarkable happened.

No championships.

No major accomplishments.

Just a weekend full of ordinary moments.

But laying here tonight, those are the things I’m thinking about.

Kids and parents playing baseball together.

Little hands reaching for leaves.

An air hockey table that looked enormous from three feet off the ground.

A day at the lake with good friends.

And the realization that I’m actually noticing these moments while they’re happening.

For a long time, I worried I was missing them.

This weekend felt like a little proof that maybe I’m starting to slow down enough to see them.
